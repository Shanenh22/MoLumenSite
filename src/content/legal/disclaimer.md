## Perspective, not certainty

Astrology, as practiced here, offers perspective: pattern recognition, timing, reflection, and a structured vocabulary for thinking about your life. It does not offer certainty, and no reading predicts a fixed future. There is fate and there is choice — and every reading is built on the conviction that the choices are yours.

## You remain the author

Nothing in a reading removes your agency or responsibility. A chart describes tendencies and seasons; you decide what to do with them. Any astrologer who tells you the stars *require* a particular decision has stepped outside ethical practice — and outside what we do here.

## Not professional services

Readings are not medical, psychological, legal, or financial services, and are not a substitute for care from licensed professionals. If you are facing a health crisis, mental-health emergency, legal matter, or financial decision requiring licensed advice, please consult the appropriate professional. Mo will tell you the same in a reading.

## Legal classification

In some jurisdictions, astrology services are legally required to be classified as entertainment. Where that applies, readings are provided on that basis, for entertainment and educational purposes.

## Adults 18+

Readings and services are intended for adults 18 years and older.
