Most disclaimers exist to protect the person who wrote them. This one is here because being clear
about what astrology can and cannot do is part of practising it honestly, and because the
difference matters most to the people who arrive at a reading with something genuinely at stake.

## Perspective, not certainty

Astrology, as practised here, offers perspective: pattern recognition, timing, reflection, and a
structured vocabulary for thinking about your life. It does not offer certainty, and no reading
predicts a fixed future.

A chart describes conditions and seasons, which themes are active, when doors tend to open, where
friction is likely. It does not describe an outcome, because the outcome depends on what you do.
There is fate and there is choice, and every reading here is built on the conviction that the
choices are yours.

## You remain the author of your life

Nothing in a reading removes your agency or your responsibility. A chart shows tendencies; you
decide what to do with them. Any astrologer who tells you the stars _require_ a particular decision
has stepped outside ethical practice, and outside what happens here.

If a reading ever leaves you feeling that your life has been decided for you, something has gone
wrong in the telling. Say so, and we will go back over it.

## Not professional services

**Readings are not medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, financial, or tax services, and are
not a substitute for care from a licensed professional.**

Mo is an astrologer. She is not a physician, therapist, attorney, or financial adviser, and she does
not diagnose conditions, recommend or discourage treatment, interpret the law, or advise on money
decisions.

If you are facing a health concern, a mental-health crisis, a legal matter, or a financial decision
that needs licensed advice, please consult the appropriate professional. Mo will tell you the same
thing in a reading, and she will tell you plainly rather than talking around it.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact your local emergency services. In the United
States, the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline can be reached by calling or texting 988.

## What a reading will not be used for

Some things are outside this practice regardless of who asks:

- Predicting death, or the death of anyone else
- Diagnosing illness, or telling you whether to take, stop, or change a medication
- Predicting pregnancy outcomes, or advising on whether to have children
- Reading the chart of a third party who has not consented, other than for a relationship reading
  the other person knows about
- Telling you to leave a marriage, a job, or a treatment
- Anything intended to influence, manipulate, or gain leverage over another person

These are not squeamishness. They are the places where astrology's genuine limits and a person's
real vulnerability meet, and where an honest practitioner declines.

## Legal classification

Readings are provided for entertainment purposes. Some jurisdictions require services of this kind
to carry that classification, and I apply it everywhere rather than case by case. You should not
have to work out which rules apply where you live in order to know what you are buying.

**That is a legal classification, not a description of the work.** This is a consulting practice,
not fortune-telling. I do not read palms, I do not contact the dead, and I do not tell you what is
going to happen to you. I prepare for every session in advance, I work from your actual chart, and
I expect you to arrive with a real question, because an hour spent on "tell me something about
myself" is an hour neither of us gets back.

Bring me something you are genuinely trying to decide or understand, and I will treat it with the
seriousness it deserves. Nothing above changes about what a reading is not.

## Educational content on this site

The reference library on this site is written to be accurate about its own foundations. Astronomical
facts are labelled as astronomical facts. Traditional doctrine is labelled as traditional doctrine.
Modern interpretation is labelled as modern interpretation, and Mo's own synthesis is labelled as
hers. Where schools of astrology genuinely disagree, the page says so rather than picking a side and
presenting it as settled.

This is not decoration. Astrology contains a great deal that is asserted confidently and agreed on
by nobody, and you are entitled to know which is which before you decide what to do with it.

Nothing in the reference library is personalised advice. It describes how the symbolism is
conventionally read; it does not know anything about you.

Readings and services are intended for adults aged 18 and over.
