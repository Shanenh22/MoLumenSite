## Our commitment

Everyone deserves a website they can actually use. This site is designed and built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA: semantic structure, full keyboard operation, visible focus indicators, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion support, meaningful alternative text, and forms with proper labels and clear errors.

## What we've done

The site works without JavaScript for all essential content; interactive tools (Reading Finder, chart explorer, sky filters) are keyboard-operable with screen-reader announcements and have non-interactive equivalents; videos load only on request; navigation is consistent, skip links are provided, and touch targets meet minimum sizes.

## Known limitations

Third-party embeds (scheduling calendar, video players) are controlled by their providers; where their accessibility falls short, alternative paths are provided (direct links, email booking). Transcripts for videos are added as they are produced and reviewed.

## Tell us

If anything on this site is difficult for you to use, please email mo@molumen.com — you'll get a human answer and, wherever possible, a fix. This statement is reviewed as the site evolves. This statement describes our target and effort; it is not a certification.
