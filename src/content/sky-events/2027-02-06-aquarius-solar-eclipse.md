An annular solar eclipse at 17° Aquarius — the Moon is too far from Earth to cover the Sun completely, so it leaves a ring of light rather than a total blackout. The path crosses the Atlantic, South America and Africa. Astrologically an eclipse new moon is read as an emphatic beginning along the house axis it touches, and Aquarius asks about the groups you belong to and the future you think you are heading toward. What eclipses do not do is decide anything for you.
