A new moon forms at 12° Cancer one day before Mercury turns direct. Two cycles are changing phase almost together, but they are not saying the same thing. The lunation opens a monthly rhythm; Mercury's station marks a turn in an apparent-motion cycle that began in Cancer and moved back into Gemini.

Cancer brings responsiveness, care, protectiveness, memory, receptivity, and tenacity. Mercury brings thought, language, exchange, and interpretation. I am interested in what happens when a feeling or memory asks to be understood without being reduced to the first explanation available.

That does not make this a universal emotional turning point. The shared sky gives us a quality to notice. The natal house, close contacts, and lived circumstances show where that quality may have something to say personally.
