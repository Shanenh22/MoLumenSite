A full moon at 1° Libra opposes the early-Aries Sun, with Mars still retrograde for another ten days. The opposition itself is the interesting structure: Aries and Libra describe two ends of a relationship between individual direction and the presence of another person.

Libra is associated with reciprocity, fairness, negotiation, aesthetics, and the work of considering more than one point of view. Aries brings initiative, separateness, and the right to act from one's own center. A full moon does not automatically produce a relationship decision, but it can give us a symbolic lens for noticing where those two needs are in conversation.

If this lunation is personally prominent, the relevant natal houses and close contacts will tell us far more than the sign pair alone.
