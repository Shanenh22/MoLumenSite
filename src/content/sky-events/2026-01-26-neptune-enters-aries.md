Neptune enters Aries, beginning a new sign chapter in a much longer planetary cycle. An ingress is a clear astronomical threshold in zodiacal longitude; astrology supplies the symbolic language used to interpret it.

Neptune is associated with ideals, imagination, longing, uncertainty, projection, and the pull toward what is difficult to define. Aries emphasizes initiative, courage, self-direction, and the pace of beginning. I would treat the combination as a long inquiry into distinguishing what inspires from what merely blurs, not as a single event that arrives on the ingress date.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Aries and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be. A collective cycle can be important without describing the same experience for everyone.
