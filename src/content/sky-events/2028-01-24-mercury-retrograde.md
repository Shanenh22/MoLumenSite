Mercury stations retrograde near 20° Aquarius and remains retrograde until February 14, 2028. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Mercury is associated with communication, information, decisions, schedules, and interpretation. In Aquarius, those themes meet groups, systems, independence, reform, friendship, and ideas about the future. This period turns my attention toward revision, clarification, and a second look.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Aquarius and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be. A collective cycle can be important without describing the same experience for everyone.
