A new moon forms at 6° Scorpio one day after Mercury turns direct. The timing is close, but I would not turn it into a promise that something suddenly resolves. The cycles overlap; they do not dictate one outcome.

Scorpio brings depth, intensity, persistence, privacy, and a desire to look beneath first impressions. A new moon here can be a useful place to notice what becomes clearer through concentration and sustained attention rather than through a quick conclusion.

What matters is whether this degree is actually active in the natal chart and what is already unfolding in the relevant house. The sign gives the lunar cycle its quality; the house shows the area of life where that quality may become more specific.
