Pluto stations retrograde at 7° Aquarius and remains retrograde until October 17. Pluto spends months of every year in apparent retrograde motion and moves only a small distance during that time. That scale matters. A Pluto station does not need to be treated as an omen simply because the symbolism attached to Pluto is weighty.

In modern astrology, Pluto is associated with power, compulsion, survival, elimination, exposure, and forms of change that reach below the surface. Aquarius adds independence, objectivity, reform-mindedness, originality, and a humanitarian perspective that can challenge assumptions about how things have always been done.

I am more interested in the long question of what changes when power meets a sign willing to detach from convention and imagine another arrangement than in predicting what happens on May 8. For an individual, the station becomes more specific through the natal house, exact contacts, and the wider timing picture.
