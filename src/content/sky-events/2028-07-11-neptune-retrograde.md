Neptune stations retrograde near 8° Aries and remains retrograde until December 16, 2028. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Neptune is associated with ideals, imagination, uncertainty, and what is difficult to define. In Aries, those themes meet initiative, courage, self-direction, and the pace of beginning. This period turns my attention toward what inspires and what still needs clearer edges.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Aries and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be.
