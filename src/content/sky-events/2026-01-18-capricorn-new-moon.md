Capricorn brings attention to structure, responsibility, authority, time, and what is built to endure. I am interested in which commitments deserve a longer horizon. That is a useful field of inquiry, not a forecast that a particular event must occur.

For a personal reading of this moment, I would look at the natal house containing 29° Capricorn, any close contacts to planets or angles, and the other timing already active.
