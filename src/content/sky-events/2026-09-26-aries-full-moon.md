A full moon places the Sun and Moon opposite one another, which is one reason astrologers often read full moons through contrast and visibility. Here the Moon is in Aries while the Sun is in Libra, bringing the self–other axis into focus.

Aries asks about initiative, directness, and the right to act from your own center. Libra asks about relationship, proportion, and the reality that our choices meet other people. I am less interested in choosing which side is "right" than in noticing what becomes clearer when both are present at once.

In your own chart, the houses occupied by Aries and Libra and any close contacts to natal planets will show where that polarity may feel more immediate. Without that context, the full moon is a shared pattern to observe rather than a promise that a particular confrontation or decision is coming.
