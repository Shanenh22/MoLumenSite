A full moon places the Sun and Moon opposite one another. Here the Moon is in Aries while the Sun is in Libra, bringing two very different ways of meeting a situation into view at once.

Aries is direct, independent, courageous, and willing to act from instinct. Libra weighs, compares, considers proportion, and looks for a fair or graceful way to hold more than one perspective. I am less interested in choosing which side is "right" than in noticing what becomes clearer when decisive action and thoughtful consideration are both present.

In your own chart, the houses occupied by Aries and Libra and any close contacts to natal planets show where that polarity may feel more immediate. The signs describe the quality of the tension; the houses show the area of life in which it may be experienced.
