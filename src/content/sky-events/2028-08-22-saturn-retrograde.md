Saturn stations retrograde near 11° Taurus and remains retrograde until January 5, 2029. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Saturn is associated with responsibility, limits, structure, and the work required to make something durable. Taurus adds questions of stability, resources, material priorities, and a sustainable pace. This period turns my attention toward what can be sustained and what needs firmer definition.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Taurus and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be.
