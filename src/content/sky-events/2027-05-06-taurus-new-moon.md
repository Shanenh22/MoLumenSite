A new moon forms at 16° Taurus two days before Pluto stations retrograde in Aquarius. Pluto belongs to a much slower cycle, so I would not turn the closeness of the dates into a dramatic story. It is simply another piece of the sky surrounding this lunation.

Taurus brings steadiness, patience, consistency, embodiment, and an appreciation for what can be experienced directly and sustained over time. Its fixed quality can support endurance, but it can also make change feel more disruptive than it needs to be.

There is a difference between continuity and immobility. What deserves patient cultivation, and what has remained unchanged mainly because changing course would be uncomfortable? A new moon marks a lunar-cycle beginning; personal relevance comes from where 16° Taurus falls in the natal chart and what it contacts.
