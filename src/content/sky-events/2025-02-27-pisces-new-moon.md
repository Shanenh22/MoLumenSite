*From the archive. Originally published February 27, 2025.*

I kept thinking about a busy train station when I wrote about this New Moon: people arriving, leaving, waiting, changing direction. Some transitions are chosen. Others announce themselves before we feel ready. Either way, there is a moment when staying exactly where we were is no longer an option.

That was the feeling I associated with this New Moon in Pisces. Endings and beginnings were close together, and the difficult part was not necessarily knowing that something was changing. It was allowing enough room for the change to be real.

"Let go or be dragged" had become a phrase I used with myself. I know the difference between persistence and clinging only because I have confused the two plenty of times. Sometimes we stay attached to what is familiar because uncertainty feels harder than dissatisfaction.

But space is not the same as emptiness. Sometimes it is simply the part of a transition where the next shape has not become visible yet.

## The astrology of this new moon

The New Moon occurred February 27, 2025, at 6:44 CST, at 9°40′ Pisces. Saturn and Venus were also moving through Pisces at roughly 20°–24°, while Neptune and the North Node were near 28°. Jupiter squared the Pisces placements, most closely Saturn, adding tension to an already mutable picture.

I read that combination as a strong transition signature: several parts of the chart were asking what could continue in its present form and what required adjustment. Pisces symbolism can describe dissolution, permeability, imagination, and release, but those themes do not tell everyone to "surrender" in the same way. The chart still has to meet the life.

## Looking back at the cycle

At the time, I suggested creating some quiet around the questions instead of rushing to answer them. Step away from the noise long enough to notice what you were trying to preserve, what was already changing, and whether the two were still compatible.

A new moon marks the start of a cycle, but the meaning becomes clearer with time. If you are revisiting this one now, look to the part of your chart where Pisces falls and ask what was beginning, ending, or changing around late February 2025. What did you think the transition was about then, and what does hindsight show you now?
