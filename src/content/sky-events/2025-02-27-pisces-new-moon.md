*From the archive — originally published February 27, 2025.*

I find myself in a busy train station, where the air hums with movement and emotions flicker across faces — anticipation, farewell, quiet determination. People surge forward, purposeful yet fleeting. Some transitions are expected; others catch me off guard. But the current carries us forward all the same.

This New Moon in Pisces stirs up the bittersweet season of endings — personal, collective, and everything in between. Let's be honest: endings are rarely easy. We want to hold on, stretch time, keep both feet in the past while reaching for the future. But life doesn't work that way. At some point, we have to loosen our grip, release what no longer feeds us, and trust that new possibilities will take root.

"Let go or be dragged" has become my motto. I've been dragged plenty, mistaking sheer tenacity for loyalty, and persistence for wisdom. Sometimes we cling to what we have not because it serves us, but because we fear what might take its place.

But here's the thing: space isn't emptiness. It's an opening. A pause before renewal.

## The astrology of this new moon

This New Moon arrived February 27, 2025, at 6:44 CST, landing at 9°40′ Pisces — a moment thick with transition. Saturn and Venus drifted through Pisces at 20°–24°, while Neptune and the North Node lingered at 28°, stirring a deep current of mutable energy. Jupiter squared these Pisces placements — most intensely Saturn — signaling a parting of ways: a break from what no longer aligns, and a realignment with deeper integrity.

Pisces, the water sign that dissolves boundaries, reminds us that surrender is not weakness — it's power in motion.

## How to honor a moon like this

Astrologers look for repeating patterns, and this chart's was clear: let go, recalibrate, trust the flow. Find stillness. Step away from distractions. Let your thoughts drift, like leaves on a slow-moving stream, until clarity surfaces. Release what no longer fits, and make room for the next version of your life.

New moons always mark fresh starts. After the dust settles, tune in — especially to the area of your natal chart where the new moon lands. A new chapter opens. Where will it take you?
