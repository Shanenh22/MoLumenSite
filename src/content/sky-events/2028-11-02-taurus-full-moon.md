The full moon falls at about 10° Taurus. A full moon brings the Sun and Moon into opposition, and astrology uses that phase as a symbolic point of contrast, culmination, or clearer awareness.

Taurus brings attention to stability, resources, priorities, value, and pace. I am interested in what has become dependable through repetition and where steadiness may now reveal something that was harder to evaluate while it was still changing. The useful question is not whether everything should stay the same, but whether the current arrangement still supports what matters.

For a personal reading of this moment, the natal house containing 10° Taurus, close contacts to planets or angles, and the wider timing picture would add specificity. The shared sky offers the pattern; the individual chart supplies the context.
