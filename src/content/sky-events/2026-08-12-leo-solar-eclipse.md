A solar eclipse is already an arresting piece of astronomy: the Sun, Moon, and Earth briefly aligning so precisely that the Moon can obscure the Sun from our point of view. Astrology adds another layer by asking what the eclipse's zodiacal position may symbolize.

In Leo, I am interested in questions of visibility, creativity, authorship, pride, and the willingness to put something of yourself into the world. An eclipse can make that part of the sky worth watching more closely, but I would not treat it as an instruction to reinvent yourself or as a guarantee that something dramatic must happen.

The personal question begins with your chart. Which house contains this degree of Leo? Does the eclipse closely contact a natal planet or angle? What other timing is active, and what is already unfolding in your life? Those details are what turn a shared sky event into an individual conversation.
