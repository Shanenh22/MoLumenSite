A full moon at 26° Capricorn is catalogued as a very slight penumbral lunar eclipse. The visible effect is minimal, which makes the contrast between astronomical scale and astrological category worth noticing.

Capricorn brings discipline, structure, responsibility, restraint, time, and endurance into opposition with Cancer's responsiveness, care, protectiveness, memory, and receptivity. The signs describe two different qualities rather than two predetermined areas of life.

Personal meaning depends on the natal houses involved, exact contacts to the lunation, and the wider timing picture.
