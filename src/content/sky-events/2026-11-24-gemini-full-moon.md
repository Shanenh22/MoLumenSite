The Moon is full in Gemini opposite the Sun in Sagittarius, which puts two ways of making sense of experience across from one another. Gemini gathers, compares, asks, and keeps information moving. Sagittarius looks for the larger pattern and the meaning that can hold those pieces together.

I like this axis because both sides are useful and both can become distorted. Facts without a larger frame can become noise; conviction without enough information can become certainty too soon. This full moon offers a good moment to notice which side of that conversation has more room in your thinking right now.

A full moon does not guarantee that a conversation reaches a climax or that hidden information appears. Its personal significance depends on the houses and natal contacts involved. The shared sky gives us a question; your chart and lived circumstances tell us whether it is one you are actually answering.
