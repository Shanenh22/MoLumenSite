A new moon begins another lunar cycle, and in Virgo the symbolism turns our attention toward the small things that make larger systems function: discernment, practice, maintenance, craft, and the difference between useful refinement and endless correction.

I do not think every new moon needs a resolution attached to it. Sometimes the more interesting use of a Virgo lunation is simply to notice what is asking for better care. What has become unnecessarily complicated? What works because someone keeps tending it? What detail has been easy to dismiss but is quietly affecting the whole?

In a personal chart, the house containing this new moon and any close natal contacts tell us where those questions may become more specific. Without that context, this remains what it is: a shared cycle and a useful lens, not a personalized instruction.
