A new moon begins another lunar cycle, and in Virgo the symbolism turns our attention toward discernment, precision, craft, analysis, and the desire to make something more coherent or useful.

Virgo can notice what is out of place and what could be refined, but refinement can become endless if every imperfection is treated as a problem. I am interested in the difference between careful discrimination and the urge to keep correcting after enough has already been learned.

In a personal chart, the house containing this new moon and any close natal contacts tell us where those qualities become more specific. The sign describes how the lunation operates; the house shows the area of life in which it may be experienced.
