Mercury stations retrograde near 23° Pisces and remains retrograde until March 20, 2026. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Mercury is associated with communication, information, decisions, schedules, and the way we interpret what we hear. In Pisces, those themes meet imagination, compassion, uncertainty, meaning, rest, and what cannot be solved by analysis alone. This period turns my attention toward revision, clarification, and a second look.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Pisces and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be. A collective cycle can be important without describing the same experience for everyone.
