Mars turns retrograde in Virgo on 10 January and stays that way until 1 April, retreating into Leo along the way — the longest retrograde of the year and the only Mars retrograde until 2029. Traditionally this is the period for revising rather than launching: energy that goes out tends to come back for a second pass. In practice most people notice it as effort that does not convert, and the useful response is patience with your own pace rather than force.
