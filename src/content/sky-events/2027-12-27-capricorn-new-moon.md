The last new moon of 2027, at 6° Capricorn, four days after Saturn turns direct in Aries. Capricorn is Saturn's own sign, so this is a beginning under the sign of consequence — the kind of resolution that survives February. A fitting close to a year with almost no sign changes in it: less about crossing thresholds than about what you did with the ground you were already standing on.
