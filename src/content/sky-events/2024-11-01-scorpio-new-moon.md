*From the archive. Originally published November 1, 2024.*

Some lunations feel less like a clean beginning than a point of pressure: something has reached the stage where continuing to carry it takes more effort than changing it.

That was the quality I was watching in this Scorpio New Moon. It arrived during a turbulent stretch, with consequential choices creating tension both personally and collectively. A new moon is a beginning, but beginnings do not always feel gentle. Sometimes they begin with the recognition that an old way of acting, responding, or holding on has run its course.

## The astrology

The chart carried a rare Kite formation involving Mars and Pluto, the traditional and modern rulers of Scorpio. For me, that concentrated the question around action and transformation: not change for its own sake, but a closer look at what our choices are reinforcing and what might need to be done differently.

Scorpio symbolism often brings our attention to what has become difficult to ignore. That does not tell any one person what must happen next. It does invite a more candid question: what are you continuing to carry because it is familiar, even if it no longer belongs with where you are going?

The same question has a collective dimension. Individual choices do not happen in isolation. How we act toward one another contributes to the atmosphere we all live in. In uncertain times, authenticity and human connection matter precisely because they ask us to remain conscious of one another's humanness.

## Looking back at the cycle

At the time, I suggested making room for stillness: spiritual practice, breathwork, meditation, or another way of quieting the mind enough to notice what was underneath the noise. The point was not to force an answer. It was to become more able to recognize one.

If you are revisiting this lunation now, the useful question is retrospective: what was beginning around November 2024 in the part of your chart where Scorpio falls, and what do you understand about that beginning now that you could not have known then?
