*From the archive — originally published November 1, 2024.*

Can you feel the energy burning off what you no longer need to carry? Can you trust the power of the flame?

This was a turbulent stretch, with many high-stakes choices creating tension. The Scorpio New Moon offered another beginning — a fresh start or a re-start, depending on what held priority in your life.

## The astrology

The chart carried a rare Kite formation involving Mars and Pluto — the traditional and modern rulers of Scorpio. The call: transform our actions and our expression, and begin anew.

Each of us possesses the power to make choices that align with the life we truly desire. Our individual actions shape our collective experience. In uncertain times, authenticity and human connection are the antidotes — what can we do, as individuals, to cherish our own and other people's humanness?

## Working with the cycle

For the 28-day lunar cycle that followed, the counsel was simple: spiritual practice, breathwork, meditation — quiet the mind, because it can get even louder. Set intentions where the new moon fell in your own chart, and check progress at the quarter moons.
