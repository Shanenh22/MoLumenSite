Mercury stations retrograde at 6° Pisces, crosses back into Aquarius, and turns direct on March 3 at 21° Aquarius. All three Mercury retrogrades in 2027 cross a sign boundary backward, giving us two different symbolic environments for the same Mercury cycle.

Mercury is associated with thinking, language, learning, exchange, naming, and interpretation. Pisces adds imagination, receptivity, ambiguity, symbolism, and sensitivity to what is not easily defined. Aquarius brings independence, objectivity, originality, and a willingness to question established assumptions.

What interests me is how thought changes when a question moves between impression and detachment, or when an unconventional idea has to make room for information that does not fit it neatly. If this cycle is personally prominent, the houses involved and close contacts to natal planets or angles show where that process may have particular relevance.
