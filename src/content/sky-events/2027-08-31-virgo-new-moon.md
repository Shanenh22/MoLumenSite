A new moon forms at 8° Virgo with Jupiter now moving through the same sign. I like this pairing because Jupiter's instinct to enlarge meets Virgo's instinct to discriminate. One asks what more might be possible; the other asks which part is actually useful.

Virgo brings discernment, precision, analysis, craft, and the desire to refine what could work better. Jupiter brings growth, confidence, meaning, opportunity, and sometimes excess. Put them together and improvement becomes a more interesting question than expansion for its own sake.

The new moon opens a lunar cycle around those qualities, but it does not prescribe a new regimen or project. Its personal relevance depends on the natal house, exact contacts, and the circumstances already asking for attention.
