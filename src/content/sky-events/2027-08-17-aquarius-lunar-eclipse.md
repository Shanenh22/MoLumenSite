A penumbral lunar eclipse occurs at 24° Aquarius, two weeks after the solar eclipse in Leo. The visual character is quieter because the Moon moves through the lighter outer part of Earth's shadow.

Astrologically, the same two signs remain in view. Aquarius brings independence, objectivity, originality, reform-minded thinking, and a humanitarian perspective. Leo brings warmth, generosity, creative expression, pride, and personal leadership. I do not see those as competing moral choices.

I am interested in how a distinct individual contribution can serve a wider purpose, and how a broad, inclusive perspective can still respect individual difference. The houses involved and close contacts to 24° Aquarius or Leo show where that relationship may become personally important.
