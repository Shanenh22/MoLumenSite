For the second month in a row, the full moon falls in Scorpio. April's was near the beginning of the sign; this one is at 29°, close to its end. Two consecutive full moons on the same Taurus–Scorpio axis are unusual enough that I would keep both in view rather than treat May as an entirely separate moment.

Astrological traditions often give the final degree of a sign extra emphasis. That can be useful context, but I would not turn 29° into a guaranteed ending or deadline. What we can observe without forcing a story is the repetition itself: Taurus's steadiness, patience, and continuity are again opposite Scorpio's intensity, depth, persistence, and desire to look beneath appearances.

If April raised a recognizable question, May may offer another angle on it. Or the two lunations may describe completely different circumstances. The natal houses and close contacts tell us whether the repeated sign pattern is also repeating in a meaningful area of one person's life.
