This is the second Scorpio full moon in two months, this time at 29°, after April's full moon near the beginning of the sign. That repetition is unusual enough to make the Taurus–Scorpio axis worth watching across both lunations.

Late degrees are sometimes given special emphasis in astrological traditions, but I would not turn 29° into an automatic ending. What we can say is that the same symbolic territory returns: what is held personally and what is shared, security and exchange, intimacy, resources, trust, and the consequences of merging with other people.

If April and May do form a recognizable sequence in someone's life, the natal chart can help explain why. The sky offers the repeated pattern; lived experience tells us whether there is actually a story connecting the two.
