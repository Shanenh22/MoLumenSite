For the second month in a row, the full moon falls in Scorpio. April's was near the beginning of the sign; this one is at 29°, close to its end. Two consecutive full moons on the same Taurus–Scorpio axis are unusual enough that I would keep both in view rather than treat May as an entirely separate moment.

Astrological traditions often give the final degree of a sign extra emphasis. That can be useful context, but I would not turn 29° into a guaranteed ending, deadline, or point of no return. What we can observe without forcing a story is the repetition itself. The same symbolic territory has come back into focus: personal resources and shared resources, stability and exchange, possession and trust, what we hold for ourselves and what becomes complicated because another person is involved.

If April raised a recognizable question, May may give us another angle on it. Or the two lunations may describe completely different circumstances. Astrology should leave room for both possibilities.

The natal chart is what tells us whether this repetition has particular personal weight. If both lunations contact important points or activate the same houses, the sequence becomes more interesting. The sky repeats the pattern. Lived experience tells us whether there is actually one story running through it.
