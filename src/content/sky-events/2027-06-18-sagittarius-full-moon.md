A full moon at 28° Sagittarius stands opposite the Gemini Sun near the solstice while Mercury is retrograde. With so much attention already gathering around Mercury and Gemini, I am especially interested in what the Sagittarius side of the axis contributes.

Gemini collects the pieces: facts, questions, comparisons, contradictions, and alternate possibilities. Sagittarius looks for the larger frame that makes those pieces mean something. It is associated with belief, interpretation, philosophy, direction, and the stories through which we organize what we know. We need both. Information without a frame can remain scattered; a compelling frame without enough information can become certainty far too quickly.

That tension feels worth examining. What do you actually know, and what have you concluded from it? Which fact complicates the story you prefer? Conversely, when does a pile of information need a larger question before it becomes useful? Wonder lives comfortably in the space between those two movements: looking closely and looking broadly.

A full moon does not guarantee a revelation or final answer. Whether this one has particular personal importance depends on the houses holding 28° Gemini–Sagittarius, close natal contacts, and what is already developing there. The axis asks us to examine the relationship between information and meaning. It does not tell us which conclusion to choose.
