Saturn stations retrograde at 28° Aries and remains retrograde until December 23. Saturn makes this apparent turn every year, so the station is not evidence that life has suddenly become blocked. It is a timing marker inside a much longer passage through Aries.

Saturn asks about limits, responsibility, structure, authority, time, and consequence. Aries begins, separates, asserts, and acts. Neptune is moving through the same sign, bringing imagination, ideals, ambiguity, and permeability into a field Saturn approaches very differently. I find the coexistence more revealing than any single station date: how do you give form to an impulse without strangling it, and how do you test an ideal without reducing it to what is already familiar?

If Saturn is making a close natal contact, the retrograde may mark another pass through a question already in motion. Otherwise, this belongs mostly to the background rhythm of the year.
