Saturn stations retrograde at 28° Aries and remains retrograde until December 23. Because Saturn spends several months retrograde every year, the station itself is part of its normal cycle rather than a sign that everything becomes obstructed.

Saturn is associated with limits, responsibility, time, structure, authority, and consequence. Aries brings initiative, assertion, independence, and beginning. Neptune is also moving through Aries, adding a very different set of symbols around ideals, imagination, ambiguity, and dissolution.

I am more interested in that longer coexistence than in assigning one event to the station date. If Saturn is making a close natal contact, the retrograde can mark another pass through an already active question. Otherwise it may be best understood as part of the year's background.
