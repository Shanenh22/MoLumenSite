The full moon at 2° Leo faces the Sun in Aquarius while Mars is retrograde. A full moon is an opposition by definition, so the contrast between the two signs is part of the picture rather than an invitation to read either sign alone.

Leo brings warmth, generosity, creative expression, pride, and the willingness to lead from a personal center. Aquarius brings independence, objectivity, reform-minded thinking, and a humanitarian, inclusive perspective that can step back from the purely personal. Neither side cancels the other.

I am interested in how individual leadership can contribute to something larger without losing its heart, and how a broader perspective can remain inclusive without flattening individual difference. Mars retrograde adds a background question about effort and direction, but it does not tell everyone to hold back.

Whether this lunation is personally important depends on where 2° Leo and Aquarius fall in the natal chart, what they closely contact, and what is already unfolding there. The signs describe the quality of the opposition; the houses show where that quality may be lived.
