Aquarius brings attention to groups, systems, independence, reform, friendship, and ideas about the future. I am interested in where belonging and autonomy need to coexist. That is a useful field of inquiry, not a forecast that a particular event must occur.

For a personal reading of this moment, I would look at the natal house containing 13° Aquarius, any close contacts to planets or angles, and the other timing already active.
