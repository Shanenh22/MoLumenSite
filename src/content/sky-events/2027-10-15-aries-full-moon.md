A full moon at 22° Aries opposes the Libra Sun while Saturn and Neptune are also moving through Aries and Mercury is retrograde. Several cycles overlap, but they do not need to produce one combined storyline.

Aries brings initiative, directness, independence, courage, and willingness to act. Libra weighs, compares, seeks proportion, and considers more than one perspective. Saturn adds consequence and structure, while Neptune adds imagination and uncertainty.

When is decisive action useful, and when would another perspective improve the judgment? The natal houses, exact contacts to 22° Aries or Libra, and lived circumstances determine whether the full moon is personally significant.
