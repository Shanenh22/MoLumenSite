A full moon at 22° Aries, in the sign holding both Saturn and Neptune, while Mercury is retrograde. The Aries–Libra axis is self against other, and a full moon here tends to make the difference between the two very clear. With Saturn nearby, the honest version is more useful than the satisfying one.
