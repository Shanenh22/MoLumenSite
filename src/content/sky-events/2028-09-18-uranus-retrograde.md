Uranus stations retrograde near 14° Gemini and remains retrograde until February 16, 2029. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Uranus is associated with innovation, independence, surprise, and departures from established patterns. In Gemini, those themes meet information, language, learning, exchange, and the way ideas move between people. This period turns my attention toward what is changing form and what still needs experimentation.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Gemini and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be.
