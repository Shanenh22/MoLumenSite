Mercury stations retrograde at 6° Cancer, crosses back into Gemini, and turns direct on July 4 at 27° Gemini. The sign change gives us two different symbolic settings for the same Mercury cycle.

Cancer is responsive, protective, receptive, tenacious, and strongly connected with memory. Gemini approaches experience through curiosity, language, information, comparison, and exchange. Watching Mercury move between them draws attention to how felt experience and memory become language.

Different words can reveal another layer of a familiar story, while additional information can complicate what felt settled. If this cycle is personally important, the natal houses Mercury crosses and any close contacts show where that process may be especially relevant.
