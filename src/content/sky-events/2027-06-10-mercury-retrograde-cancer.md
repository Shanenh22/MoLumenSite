Mercury stations retrograde at 6° Cancer and slips back into Gemini, turning direct on 4 July at 27° Gemini. The second of the year, and the same backwards border-crossing as the first, a review that starts with how something feels and ends with what was actually said. Family conversations and old correspondence are the traditional territory.
