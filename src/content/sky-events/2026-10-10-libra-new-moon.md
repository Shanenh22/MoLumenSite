The Sun and Moon meet in Libra while Venus is retrograde, which makes the symbolism unusually concentrated around relationship, balance, agreement, and value. Libra is often reduced to harmony, but real balance is more interesting than keeping everything pleasant. It requires noticing what each side is carrying and whether an arrangement still reflects what matters.

Because Venus is retrograde, I would treat this less as a command to begin something new and more as a useful point of observation. What are you reconsidering about partnership, reciprocity, fairness, beauty, money, or preference? What has changed enough that an old agreement deserves another look?

This is the shared sky, not a personal forecast. The house Libra occupies in your chart, any close contacts to natal planets or angles, and the circumstances you are actually living determine which of these themes, if any, become personally important.
