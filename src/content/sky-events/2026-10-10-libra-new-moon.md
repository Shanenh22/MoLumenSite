The Sun and Moon meet in Libra while Venus is retrograde, which makes the symbolism unusually concentrated around questions of value and how we weigh competing considerations. Libra is often reduced to harmony, but its character is more active than simply keeping everything pleasant. It compares, balances, considers proportion, and looks for a fair or graceful arrangement among different factors.

Because Venus is retrograde, I would treat this less as a command to begin something new and more as a useful point of observation. What are you reconsidering about value, attraction, beauty, money, preference, or the way you decide what deserves your agreement? What has changed enough that your previous judgment deserves another look?

This is the shared sky, not a personal forecast. The house Libra occupies in your chart, any close contacts to natal planets or angles, and the circumstances you are actually living determine which of these themes, if any, become personally important.
