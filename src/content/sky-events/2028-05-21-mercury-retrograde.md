Mercury stations retrograde near 16° Gemini and remains retrograde until June 14, 2028. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Mercury is associated with communication, information, decisions, schedules, and interpretation. In Gemini, those themes meet information, language, learning, exchange, and the way ideas move between people. This period turns my attention toward revision, clarification, and a second look.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Gemini and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be. A collective cycle can be important without describing the same experience for everyone.
