A new moon forms at 7° Libra, eight days before Mercury stations retrograde in Scorpio. I would not use that approaching station to create a deadline. Astrology becomes less useful when every date is sorted into "safe" and "unsafe."

Libra weighs, compares, considers proportion, and looks for fairness and balance without requiring every difference to disappear. Its air quality can bring perspective, while its cardinal quality is capable of initiating an adjustment rather than merely maintaining peace.

What interests me is balance as an ongoing act of judgment: what deserves equal weight, what does not, and what changes when another angle is considered seriously? The natal house, close contacts, and actual circumstances show where those qualities may become personally relevant.
