The first new moon of 2027 falls at 17° Capricorn, only three days before Mars stations retrograde. I like the tension in that timing. One cycle is beginning just as another planet appears to slow almost to a standstill before reversing direction. Nothing about that combination says life must stop. It does make the question of **how** we move forward more interesting.

Capricorn is concerned with structure, responsibility, time, endurance, and what can hold its shape over the long haul. Mars brings effort, desire, assertion, and the use of force. Together they turn my attention toward the architecture behind an intention. Is there enough support under what you want to build? Is the pace sustainable? Does the effort belong to the goal, or has effort itself become the goal?

A new moon gives astrologers a symbolic beginning point, not a command to launch something. Its importance in an individual chart depends on where 17° Capricorn falls, what it closely contacts, and what is already taking shape in that part of life. The shared sky offers the pattern. The chart and the lived story tell us what, if anything, it is describing for you.
