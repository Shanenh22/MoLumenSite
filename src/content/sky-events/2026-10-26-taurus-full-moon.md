The Moon is full in Taurus opposite the Sun in Scorpio, putting two very different kinds of security across from one another. Taurus is associated with what can be sustained, possessed, touched, and relied upon. Scorpio turns attention toward what is shared, entangled, private, or difficult to control completely.

That makes this full moon less interesting to me as a prediction of a "culmination" than as a moment to notice the relationship between keeping and sharing, stability and change, simplicity and complexity. Those themes can show up through money and resources, but they can also describe the way we respond when something familiar is being asked to evolve.

Whether this full moon matters strongly for you depends on where Taurus and Scorpio fall in your chart and whether the lunation makes close contact with natal planets or angles. The shared symbolism gives us a pattern to watch; your chart and circumstances tell us where that pattern becomes personal.
