The Moon is full in Taurus opposite the Sun in Scorpio, placing two fixed signs across from one another. Taurus favors steadiness, continuity, patience, and a direct relationship with the tangible world. Scorpio brings intensity, depth, persistence, and a desire to look beneath appearances.

I am interested in the contrast between steadiness and intensity, simplicity and complexity, and preservation and change. Because both signs are fixed, neither changes direction casually. The useful question is not which sign is right, but what deserves consistency and what deserves a deeper examination.

Whether this full moon matters strongly for you depends on where Taurus and Scorpio fall in your chart and whether the lunation makes close contact with natal planets or angles. The signs describe the quality of the tension; the houses help show where in life it may be experienced.
