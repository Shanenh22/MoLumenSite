The total solar eclipse at 10° Leo, and one of the longest of the century — over six minutes of totality along a path crossing southern Spain, North Africa, Egypt and the Arabian peninsula. Astronomically this is the event of the year by a wide margin. Astrologically it is an eclipse new moon in Leo, a year after the Leo eclipse of August 2026, continuing a series along the same axis: heart, creativity, and being seen for what you actually are rather than what you produce.
