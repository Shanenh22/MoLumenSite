A full moon at 23° Pisces opposite the Virgo Sun, on the same day Uranus stations. The Virgo–Pisces axis is the one about what can be fixed and what can only be accepted, and full moons here are usually about telling the difference. Traditionally Jupiter rules Pisces, and Jupiter is currently in the opposite sign.
