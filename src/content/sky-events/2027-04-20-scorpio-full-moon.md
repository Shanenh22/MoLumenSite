A full moon at the first degree of Scorpio stands opposite the Taurus Sun. Here the axis itself tells us where to look: what is held personally and what is shared, what feels secure because it is ours and what asks us to enter an exchange with someone else.

Taurus is associated with money, possessions, material resources, steadiness, and what can be relied upon. Scorpio brings shared resources, intimacy, debt, inheritance, trust, secrecy, power, loss, and the complicated territory created when lives and resources become entangled. Scorpio is often reduced to "intensity," but that word can hide more than it explains.

I am more interested in the practical and emotional questions underneath it. What changes when something cannot be managed entirely on your own terms? What deserves to be protected, and what requires enough trust to be shared? Where does holding tightly create stability, and where does it prevent an exchange that matters?

A Scorpio full moon does not promise a revelation, confrontation, or crisis. Its personal importance depends on the houses containing the Taurus–Scorpio axis, close natal contacts to the lunation, and the circumstances already present. The symbolism can describe many stories. The chart helps narrow which one belongs to the person in front of us.
