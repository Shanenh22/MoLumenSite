A full moon at the first degree of Scorpio stands opposite the Taurus Sun. Taurus favors steadiness, continuity, patience, and a direct relationship with the tangible world. Scorpio brings intensity, depth, persistence, and a desire to look beneath appearances.

Both are fixed signs, so neither changes course lightly. That makes the contrast less about one side holding and the other sharing, and more about how steadiness meets depth, how simplicity meets complexity, and when persistence supports something versus when it keeps us locked into a position.

A Scorpio full moon does not promise a revelation, confrontation, or crisis. Its personal importance depends on the houses containing Taurus and Scorpio, close natal contacts to the lunation, and the circumstances already present. The signs describe the qualities in tension; the chart shows where those qualities are operating.
