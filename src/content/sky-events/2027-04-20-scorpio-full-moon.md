A full moon at the first degree of Scorpio opposes the Taurus Sun. The opposition puts two ends of the same axis in view: what is mine and what is shared, stability and change, possession and exchange, what is held close and what requires trust.

Scorpio has a reputation for intensity, but intensity is not an event prediction. Astrologically the sign is associated with depth, shared resources, intimacy, power, secrecy, loss, and regeneration. Taurus brings material steadiness, possessions, resources, and what is tangible enough to rely on.

I would use this lunation to notice the relationship between those symbols rather than expect a confrontation or revelation. Its personal importance depends on the natal houses and exact contacts.
