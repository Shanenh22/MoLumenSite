The new moon falls at about 5° Taurus, less than two weeks after Saturn enters the same sign. A new moon gives astrologers a symbolic beginning point, while the nearby ingress adds a longer question about patience, consistency, and what deserves continued effort.

Taurus brings attention to stability, resources, priorities, value, and pace. I am interested in what is worth tending consistently enough to become reliable. Beginning slowly is not the same as lacking direction, especially when the aim is something that can continue after the initial enthusiasm passes.
