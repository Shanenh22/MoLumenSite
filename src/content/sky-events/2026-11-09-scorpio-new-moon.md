The Sun and Moon meet in Scorpio while both Mercury and Venus are still retrograde. Scorpio brings depth, intensity, persistence, and a tendency to look beyond the obvious explanation rather than stop at first impressions.

Mercury and Venus retrograde already place communication, values, attraction, money, and relationship under review. Scorpio adds a more concentrated style to that process. I am less interested in promising a sudden breakthrough than in noticing what becomes clearer after sustained attention.

The lunation itself does not tell us which story belongs to you. Its house, close natal contacts, and the circumstances already unfolding in your life are what turn shared symbolism into personal astrology.
