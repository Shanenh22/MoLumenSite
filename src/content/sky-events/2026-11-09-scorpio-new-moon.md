The Sun and Moon meet in Scorpio while both Mercury and Venus are still retrograde. Scorpio is associated with what becomes complicated when lives, resources, loyalties, and vulnerabilities are intertwined. It is also a sign that tends to ask for more than a surface explanation.

With two personal planets preparing to station direct only a few days later, I would not read this as a promise that anything suddenly "gets moving." I am more interested in what has become clearer through revision. A conversation may need more honesty. A financial or relational arrangement may look different after closer examination. Or the important development may be internal: recognizing what you are actually attached to and why.

The lunation itself does not tell us which story belongs to you. Its house, close natal contacts, and the circumstances already unfolding in your life are what turn shared symbolism into personal astrology.
