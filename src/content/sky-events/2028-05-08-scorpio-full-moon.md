The full moon falls at about 19° Scorpio. A full moon brings the Sun and Moon into opposition, and astrology uses that visible phase as a symbolic point of culmination, contrast, or clearer awareness.

Scorpio brings attention to trust, shared priorities, commitments, depth, and what is not immediately obvious.
