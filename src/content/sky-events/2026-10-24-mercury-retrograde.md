Mercury stations retrograde at 20° Scorpio and returns to 5° Scorpio before turning direct on November 13, the same day Venus stations direct. The astronomy is apparent backward motion from our point of view on Earth. Astrologically, retrogrades are one way we mark a change in a planet's apparent rhythm and reconsider the themes associated with it.

In Scorpio, Mercury's symbolism meets a sign associated with investigation, privacy, depth, motive, and what lies beneath the obvious explanation. That can make this an interesting period for revisiting a conversation, researching something more thoroughly, or noticing where certainty has arrived before all the information has. It does not mean messages, travel, contracts, or technology are destined to fail.

If this cycle closely contacts your natal chart, it may become more personally noticeable. Otherwise it remains part of the background rhythm of the shared sky. The useful question is not "What will Mercury retrograde ruin?" but "What deserves a second look?"
