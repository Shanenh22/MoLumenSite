Uranus stations retrograde in Gemini, beginning a months-long apparent backward movement. With an outer planet, I am much less interested in treating the station as a dramatic day on the calendar than in noticing where a longer process may be asking for another look.

Gemini gives the symbolism a particular field: information, language, exchange, learning, movement, and the systems through which ideas travel. Uranus adds themes of disruption, invention, independence, and departures from established patterns. Put together, that gives us questions to watch rather than a prediction to announce.

Where is a way of thinking, communicating, or connecting changing form? What looks new but still needs revision? And, if Uranus is closely contacting your chart, which part of your own life is carrying that experiment? The natal house and aspects make the difference between a collective transit and a personal one.
