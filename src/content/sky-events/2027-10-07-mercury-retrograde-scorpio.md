Mercury stations retrograde at 5° Scorpio, moves back into Libra, and turns direct on October 28 at 19° Libra. The cycle crosses a sign boundary and moves through two symbolic settings.

Scorpio brings depth, intensity, persistence, and a desire to look beyond first impressions. Libra weighs, compares, seeks proportion, and considers more than one point of view. Mercury moving between them can shift the style of inquiry from concentrated examination toward comparison and reconsideration.

If this retrograde is personally important, the natal houses and close contacts show where those qualities become specific to the life being lived.
