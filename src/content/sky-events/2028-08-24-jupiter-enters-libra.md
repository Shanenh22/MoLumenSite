Jupiter enters Libra, beginning a new sign chapter in a much longer planetary cycle. An ingress is an astronomical threshold in zodiacal longitude; astrology supplies the symbolic language used to interpret it.

Jupiter is associated with growth, perspective, confidence, opportunity, and questions of scale. Libra emphasizes reciprocity, relationship, fairness, negotiation, and choices that have to account for more than one person. I would treat the combination as a long inquiry into which directions deserve more room and which have simply become bigger.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Libra and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be.
