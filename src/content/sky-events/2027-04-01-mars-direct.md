Mars turns direct in Leo, ending eleven weeks of retrograde motion that began in Virgo in January. Traditionally the station itself is the sticky part and the weeks after it are when plans start converting again. Nothing switches on at midnight — Mars spends a while re-covering ground it has already crossed before it reaches new degrees.
