Venus retrograde is less common than Mercury retrograde, which is one reason astrologers tend to watch it closely. Venus is associated with attraction, value, pleasure, exchange, aesthetics, money, and relationship; retrograde motion gives those themes a period of reconsideration rather than a universal script.

This cycle begins in Scorpio and later returns to Libra, moving through two very different Venusian contexts. Scorpio can bring questions of depth, trust, attachment, exchange, and what is shared beneath the surface. Libra brings relationship, balance, reciprocity, fairness, and how we negotiate with another person. I am interested in what becomes clearer as Venus moves back across that territory.

That does not mean an old relationship must return, a purchase must go wrong, or a commitment should automatically be postponed. Those are familiar astrology headlines, not reliable outcomes. In an individual chart, the houses and natal contacts show what part of life is actually participating in the cycle. The useful question is what you are being invited to reconsider about value and relationship, not what a retrograde is supposedly going to do to you.
