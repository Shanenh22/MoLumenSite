Venus stations retrograde near 20° Gemini and remains retrograde until June 22, 2028. Apparent retrograde motion is a normal part of the planet's cycle, so I read the station as a change of phase rather than as a warning.

In astrology, Venus is associated with values, preferences, agreements, aesthetics, and what feels worth choosing. Gemini adds information, conversation, comparison, and several possibilities held at once. The combination turns my attention toward reconsidering terms and noticing whether a preference still fits once it has been discussed more fully.

For an individual chart, the house receiving Gemini and any close contacts to natal planets or angles determine how personally relevant this shift may be.
