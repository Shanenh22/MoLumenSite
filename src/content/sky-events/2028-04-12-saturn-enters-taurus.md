Saturn enters Taurus on April 12, beginning a new sign chapter in a much longer cycle. An ingress marks a change in zodiacal position; astrology adds the symbolic framework used to consider what that change may mean.

Saturn is traditionally connected with structure, responsibility, time, and endurance. Taurus emphasizes stability, resources, priorities, value, and pace. I am interested in what becomes more workable when progress is measured over a longer horizon rather than by immediate movement.

In an individual chart, the house receiving Taurus, close contacts to natal planets or angles, and the wider timing picture determine how personally relevant this longer cycle may be.
