The fastest way to be disappointed by an astrology reading is to want the wrong things from it. The fastest way to be genuinely helped is to know what it's actually for. As someone who gives readings for a living, here is my honest accounting of both columns.

## What a reading can do

**Show you your patterns from the outside.** The chart gives us a structured way to look at your life's recurring themes, in work, love, family, money, without the usual defensiveness. It's remarkable what people can acknowledge about themselves when it arrives as "your chart suggests" instead of "your sister says."

**Name the season you're in.** Transits describe chapters: seasons of building, pruning, testing, harvesting. Knowing which one you're in, and roughly how long it runs, changes how you spend your energy. Effort aligned with its season goes further.

**Give language to what you already sensed.** Half of my job is precision: turning a vague "something's off in my career" into a specific, workable question. Clients regularly say "I knew that, but I'd never said it out loud." That's not the chart being magic. That's the chart being a very good mirror.

**Widen the option space.** A good reading usually surfaces choices you weren't seeing: not because the chart hides secrets, but because patterns, once visible, suggest their own alternatives.

**Mark timing worth considering.** Windows when a launch, conversation, or change flows more naturally, held as informed perspective, never as commandment.

## What a reading can't do, and what an ethical astrologer won't pretend

**It can't decide for you.** The chart describes conditions; it doesn't cast votes. Anyone who tells you the stars require you to quit, stay, marry, or move has left astrology for something else.

**It can't guarantee outcomes.** No chart shows "the" future. Tendencies, yes; certainties, no. Guaranteed-outcome astrology is a contradiction in terms and usually a sales funnel.

**It can't replace professionals.** Not your doctor, therapist, lawyer, or financial advisor, full stop. A responsible astrologer refers out and says so plainly. (I do, and [the disclaimer](/disclaimer/) says it in writing.)

**It can't fix anything by itself.** Insight is the beginning of change, not the completion of it. The reading hands you a clearer map; the walking is still yours.

**It can't tell you who you're allowed to be.** Charts don't contain permission slips or prison sentences. Every placement can be lived a dozen ways, which one you live is authorship, and authorship stays with you.

## The honest bottom line

A reading is an hour of structured, unhurried perspective on your own life, with a vocabulary refined over two thousand years for describing human patterns and seasons. Held that way (perspective, not prophecy), it's one of the more useful hours you can spend. Wanted as fortune-telling, it will disappoint you, and it should.
