# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO

Build a durable knowledge source, not a volume-content farm.

## Core topic clusters
- readings
- birth chart basics
- planets
- signs
- houses
- aspects
- timing/transits
- Current Sky
- lunations/eclipses
- horoscopes
- methodology/sources
- preparation

## Every publishable item should support where applicable
- unique title
- meta description
- canonical
- H1/semantic headings
- internal links
- breadcrumbs
- schema
- image dimensions/alt
- Open Graph
- truthful published/updated dates
- author/entity information

## AEO/GEO
Favor direct answers, concise definitions, entity consistency, dated/sourced facts, authorship, semantic HTML and context-rich internal links.

Do not manufacture FAQs solely for schema.
