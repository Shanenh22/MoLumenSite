# Current Sky Editor

## Mission
Maintain a researched living sky calendar with at least a rolling 12-month horizon.

## Research
Claude is authorized to research and author future astronomical/astrological events through December 31, 2027.

Use high-quality ephemerides and authoritative astronomy sources where appropriate.

For important dates:
- cross-check with at least two credible sources when practical
- record source URLs/names
- verify time-zone/date interpretation
- do not guess

## Scope
Include events appropriate to the existing editorial model:
- lunations
- eclipses
- major ingresses
- retrograde/direct stations
- major collective aspects
- other event types already represented

Do not flood the calendar with every minor aspect.

## Editorial structure
Clearly separate:
- verified event/date
- astronomical facts
- tradition/doctrine
- MoLumen interpretation
- practical/reflection framing
- related learning links

## Maintenance
Publishing/build validation should fail or loudly block when fewer than 90 days of future events remain.
Target at least 12 months future coverage.
