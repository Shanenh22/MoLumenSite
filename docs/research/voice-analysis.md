# Voice Analysis — Mo Lumen Astrology

Sources: molumen.com homepage, /about, /contact, and full blog posts (New Moon in Pisces 2025-02-27; New Moon in Scorpio 2024-11-01; Libra eclipse 2024-10-02). Quotes verbatim.

## 1. Established voice markers

**Identity claims (use as-is):**
- "Astrologer. Teacher. Writer."
- "I am your interpreter of the language of the planets."
- "a modern-day astrologer with a classical lens"
- "practical and inspiring… candid and compassionate… intuitive and skillful"

**Core philosophy (backbone of all rewritten copy):**
- "There is fate and there is choice."
- "Knowledge brings Insight. Insight brings Power. Power brings Freedom."
- Astrology helps you "actively choose what suits you best."
- Agency-first framing everywhere: "Each of us possesses the power to make choices that align with the life we truly desire."

**Signature moves observed in the writing:**
1. Opens with a sensory, first-person scene or image before any astrology (the train-station opening of the Pisces post).
2. Direct second-person questions as section pivots: "Can you trust the power of the flame?", "What does this eclipse have you balancing?"
3. Candid self-disclosure used sparingly: "'Let go or be dragged' has become my motto. I've been dragged plenty…"
4. Technical precision when it counts: exact degrees, dates, times with time zone ("February 27, 2025, at 6:44 CST, landing at 9°40' Pisces"), named configurations (Kite), rulership logic.
5. Practical closers: short imperative lists ("Find stillness. Step away from distractions."), lunar-cycle date timelines, per-rising-sign guidance.
6. Aphoristic compression: "space isn't emptiness. It's an opening. A pause before renewal."
7. Warmth with boundaries: "I'm a generous person but I won't share your email with anyone else."

## 2. Voice profile for new copy

Warm, personal, intelligent, grounded. First person from Mo. Second person to the reader. Conversational cadence but complete sentences; contractions welcome. Sensory metaphor drawn from nature, water, light, journeys — never crystal balls, spells, or cosmic-girlboss slang. Beginner-safe: every technical term explained in plain language on first use, with a link to the glossary. Experienced-reader depth: real technique named honestly (horary, synastry, composite, solar return, classical/traditional lens, NCGR/STA training).

## 3. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "This transit tends to surface…" | "This transit will make you…" |
| "a useful window for…" | "the universe wants you to…" |
| "Here's what I'd watch for" | fear-based retrograde/eclipse panic |
| name choice explicitly in every interpretive passage | deterministic verdicts, guaranteed outcomes |
| exact astronomical data, verified | manufactured urgency, countdowns, scarcity |
| candid first-person asides (rationed) | over-sharing or therapy-speak |
| "perspective, not certainty" | medical/legal/financial/mental-health claims |

## 4. Litmus test

Every page should sound like a smart, kind former science teacher who found her second vocation in classical astrology and talks to you across a kitchen table with your chart between you — never like an app, a brand, or an oracle.
