# Finishing your Cal.com booking setup

Hi Mo — there are two things left to set up in Cal.com before the new site goes live, plus one
small typo to fix. Nothing here touches the website itself; it's all inside your Cal.com account.

**Roughly 30–40 minutes.** You do the same short edit on each of your nine readings, so the first
one takes a few minutes and the rest go quickly.

Nothing you do here can break the website. If you get stuck partway through, stop — half-finished
is fine, and the site behaves the same until we switch it on.

---

## Why these two things matter

**1. The website says every reading is on Zoom or the phone. Right now the booking page doesn't
ask that — it asks people to type a location into an empty box labelled "Somewhere else."**

That box is the last thing someone sees before they enter their card details. Most people won't
know what to put in it, and a confusing question at the payment screen is where bookings get
abandoned.

**2. The website tells people they'll send you their birth details when they book. At the moment
Cal.com never asks for them.**

So two things go wrong: the client finishes paying without being asked for anything, and wonders
whether it went through properly — and you have to email every single client to chase their birth
date, time and place before you can cast a chart.

Both are quick fixes.

# Part 1 — Fix "Somewhere else" (all nine readings)

Zoom is already connected to your account, so this is just picking it.

You'll repeat this for each of your nine readings:

> Natal Chart Reading — 60 minutes · Natal Chart Reading — 90 minutes · Relationship Astrology
> Consultation · Want More Clarity — Follow-Up Reading · Want More Clarity — within 3 months ·
> Solar Return Reading · Life Changes Astrological Consultation · Quick Check-In — One Topic ·
> Monthly Personal Transits

### Steps

1. Go to **cal.com** and sign in.
2. Click **Event Types** in the left menu. Your nine readings are listed there.
3. Click the first reading.
4. You'll land on the **Setup** tab. Scroll to the **Location** section.
5. It currently says **Custom attendee location**. Click the **X** next to it to remove it.
6. Click **Add a location** → choose **Zoom Video**.
7. Click **Add a location** again → choose **Attendee phone number**.
8. Click **Save** (top right).

### What that does

Instead of an empty box, the person booking now sees a simple choice:

> **Where should we meet?**
> ○ Zoom  ○ Phone

If they pick Zoom, Cal.com creates the meeting link automatically. If they pick Phone, Cal.com
asks for *their* number and shows it to you — so you call them.

### Two things to avoid

- **Don't pick "Organizer phone number."** That publishes your own number on the booking page for
  anyone to see. "Attendee phone number" is the one you want.
- **Don't add an in-person option.** In-person DFW sessions are described on the site as something
  arranged with you directly, and adding it here would turn that into a standing self-service
  offer.

# Part 2 — Ask for birth details (three readings only)

This is the screen you already found — the **Booking questions** section with the **+ Add question**
button at the bottom.

**You only need to do this on three readings**, not all nine:

- Natal Chart Reading — 60 minutes
- Natal Chart Reading — 90 minutes
- Relationship Astrology Consultation

Your follow-up readings are for people whose charts you already have, so asking them again would be
odd. There's an optional small addition for those further down.

### How to add a question

1. **Event Types** → click the reading → click the **Advanced** tab.
2. Scroll to **Booking questions**.
3. Click **+ Add question**.
4. Fill in the **Input type**, the **Identifier/Label**, whether it's **Required**, and the
   placeholder if there is one.
5. Click **Save** on the question, then **Save** on the page.

## For BOTH natal readings — add these five

Do this on **Natal — 60 minutes**, then again on **Natal — 90 minutes**.

| # | Question (label) | Input type | Required? | Placeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Birth date | Short Text | **Yes** | `12 April 1988` |
| 2 | Do you know your birth time? | Radio | **Yes** | *(options below)* |
| 3 | Birth time, if you have it | Short Text | **No** | `3:42pm — leave blank if you're not sure` |
| 4 | Birthplace (city and country) | Short Text | **Yes** | `Austin, USA` |
| 5 | What would you most like to explore? | Long Text | No | `Two or three real questions you're living with` |

**For question 2**, choose **Radio** as the input type and add these three options exactly:

```
Yes — from a certificate or reliable record
Roughly — I know the part of the day
No, and I'm not sure where to find it
```

### Please keep question 3 optional, and keep that third radio option

This one really matters. Plenty of people genuinely don't know their birth time — there's a whole
page on your site about how to track one down, and the Reading Finder is written to reassure people
about it. If the booking form *demands* a birth time, all of that kindness ends at a hard stop on
the payment screen, and you lose a client who would have been perfectly happy to book.

Asking the question is useful. Requiring an answer is not.

## While you're on the Advanced tab — one small tidy-up

In your Booking questions list there's a stock question called **"What is this meeting about?"**

On **Quick Check-In** it's already switched off — the grey toggle and the "Hidden" tag next to it,
exactly as in the screenshot you sent. On some of the other readings it's still switched on, and
where it is, it's a *required* question.

It's a strange thing to ask someone who has just chosen "Natal Chart Reading — 90 minutes," and the
questions above replace it with something far more useful. So on each reading, if it's showing,
click its toggle to switch it off.

You can leave **Additional notes**, **Add guests** and **Reason for reschedule** exactly as they
are.

# When you're done — a two-minute check

Please do this bit, because it's the only part nobody else can test for you.

1. Open **cal.com/molumen/natal-90** in a normal browser window (or a private/incognito one).
2. Pick any available time.
3. Look at the form. You should see:
   - **Where should we meet?** with Zoom and Phone — *not* a box saying "Somewhere else"
   - Your new birth-detail questions
   - **No** "What is this meeting about?"
4. **Stop there. Don't complete the booking** — it would charge a real card and put a real
   appointment in your calendar.

If it looks right, you're finished.
