# Brand and Editorial Guide

## Voice
Warm, intelligent, grounded, candid, sophisticated, specific, conversational and agency-first.

Avoid generic AI writing, hype, clickbait, excessive bulleting, repetitive sentence patterns and formulaic conclusions.

## Astrology framing
Always preserve distinctions among astronomical fact, traditional doctrine, modern practice and Mo's interpretation.

## Agency
Avoid presenting planets as deterministic actors. Avoid doom, guarantees or certainty claims.

## Editorial quality
- Follow Chicago-style conventions unless existing project conventions differ.
- Define specialized terms.
- Keep complex astrology understandable to nonexperts.
- Prefer cohesive paragraphs.
- Use lists only when they improve comprehension.
- Link deeper material rather than overloading pages.

## Conversion copy
Prefer specific CTAs such as:
- Find Your Reading
- Compare Readings
- Book This Reading
- Meet Mo
- Join The Sky This Month
- Watch
- Explore

Avoid generic Learn More everywhere.

## Research
When outside research is authorized:
- prefer primary/authoritative sources
- cross-check dates/numbers that affect publication
- record sources
- synthesize rather than copy
