# Publish Blog

## Normal Mo workflow

Pages CMS is the default interface for routine article publishing.

1. Open **Write & Publish → Blog posts** in Pages CMS.
2. Create a new article and leave **Draft ON**.
3. Complete title, description, publish date, category, tags, and article body.
4. Write or paste the article in the rich-text editor.
5. Save freely while Draft remains ON.
6. Run **Run site quality check** before publishing.
7. Review staging for readability, links, images, mobile flow, voice, and the final next step.
8. Turn **Draft OFF** and save only when the article is ready to appear publicly.

## Editorial standard

For substantive writing, follow `MoLumen_OS/02_BRAND_AND_EDITORIAL_GUIDE.md`.

A MoLumen article should sound like warm authority without mystique: intelligent, grounded, candid, curious, technically informed in plain language, conversational, and agency-first. Preserve wonder without using science as proof of astrology. Distinguish checkable fact, astrological tradition/practice, and Mo's interpretation when that matters.

The Blog develops durable ideas and lets readers think with Mo. It should not merely duplicate an Explore reference entry, a Current Sky event, or a service page.

Choose the ending by intent: foundational learning can lead to Start Here/Explore, timing to Current Sky/Transits, a personal question to the Reading Finder, and reading-process topics to How Readings Work/Readings. Do not append Book to every article.

## Claude responsibilities

When Claude is helping with an article:

1. Use the `molumen-editor` skill for substantive research/prose and `molumen-publisher` for structure/QA.
2. Read the blog template and canonical editorial guide rather than relying on an old prompt.
3. Preserve Mo's voice, reader intent, and content-role boundaries.
4. Verify research-heavy factual claims with reliable sources.
5. Validate against the live blog schema and Pages CMS fields.
6. Add natural internal links and related content only when useful.
7. Confirm truthful metadata/schema/canonical/breadcrumb behavior and an intent-appropriate CTA.
8. Run relevant build/content/link checks.
9. Keep Pages CMS compatibility intact.

Do not require Mo to edit Astro, YAML, JSON, schema, or Git commands for an ordinary article.
