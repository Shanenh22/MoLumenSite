# Publish Video

Pages CMS is the normal owner-facing interface once the finished video exists on YouTube or Instagram.

1. Verify the real platform metadata: title, publication date, external ID/URL, thumbnail if used, and duration if known.
2. In Pages CMS open **Write & Publish → Videos** and leave **Draft ON** during setup/review.
3. Use the real YouTube video ID rather than the full URL. Never invent an ID, publication date, duration, thumbnail, transcript, or platform metadata.
4. Write a summary in Mo's current voice: warm, intelligent, grounded, conversational, and specific. The summary should say what the viewer will understand, not merely advertise the video.
5. Keep the site's privacy-conscious click-to-load facade. No YouTube request should occur before the visitor activates it.
6. Add a transcript or useful summary when available. Do not claim a full transcript if only a summary exists.
7. Add `VideoObject` structured data only from verified metadata and only when required fields are real.
8. Link related owned content when it genuinely deepens the topic. Video should support the website's knowledge ecosystem rather than replace it.
9. Choose the next step by topic: Explore for structured learning, Current Sky for an applied sky topic, Blog for a developed idea, or Reading Finder when the video naturally raises a personal question.
10. Test mobile, keyboard/focus behavior, layout shift, and no-initial-YouTube-request behavior.
11. Run relevant content/build/link checks and review staging.
12. Turn **Draft OFF** only when metadata, summary, links, and playback behavior are ready.
