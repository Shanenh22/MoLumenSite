# Publish Current Sky

Pages CMS is the normal owner-facing interface for a routine Current Sky entry. Use Claude Editor when research or substantive drafting is needed, and Claude Publisher for structure/QA.

1. Research the event with reliable ephemeris/astronomy sources.
2. Cross-check important dates when practical and verify the intended US Central/date-only interpretation.
3. Record the real source in `sourceNote` and set `lastVerified`.
4. Draft against the live sky-event fields in Pages CMS or `MoLumen_OS/templates/current-sky-template.md`; do not use retired fields.
5. Keep **Draft ON** during research/review.
6. Follow `MoLumen_OS/07_CURRENT_SKY_EDITOR.md` and the Brand and Editorial Guide.
7. Separate verified sky facts, astrological tradition/practice, and Mo's interpretation.
8. Use the sequence where useful: what is happening → what the pattern means → what Mo is noticing → a question/possibility → reader agency.
9. Keep shared-sky interpretation distinct from a personal forecast. If rising-sign guidance is included, frame it as a broad whole-sign-house lens that says where to look, not what will happen.
10. Add only useful related learning/sky links. Keep the next step sky/learning-focused unless the reader's question has clearly become personal.
11. Run the relevant content/build/link and Current Sky horizon checks.
12. Review staging, including mobile readability and any rising-sign interaction.
13. Turn **Draft OFF** only after dates, sources, copy, and links are ready.

Do not add hosted `Event` schema to an article merely because it describes a celestial event. Do not use the astronomical event date as an article publication date unless that is actually true.
