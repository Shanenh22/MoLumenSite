## Editorial checklist
- [ ] Rising sign is the structural lens; do not present Sun sign as an equivalent substitute
- [ ] Whole-sign house emphasis tells the reader where to look, not exactly what will happen
- [ ] Whole chart + timing + lived circumstances are acknowledged as the personalizing layer
- [ ] Warm, friend-to-friend, grounded voice; intelligent without sounding academic
- [ ] Planets are not written as deterministic actors
- [ ] Avoid doom, certainty, therapy language, mystical hype, and formulaic self-help endings
- [ ] Avoid tedious lists; develop one coherent scenario, at most two, when possible
- [ ] Vary sentence structure and placement of the planetary/event sentence so the set does not feel templated
- [ ] Keep the reader active; use action/choice language without turning the horoscope into instructions
- [ ] For an ingress or larger shift, keep enough emphasis on the overall change in energy/pattern rather than reducing everything to daily application
- [ ] Preserve Mo's curiosity and wonder without losing complexity or agency
- [ ] Do not invent personal predictions beyond what a general rising-sign lens can support
