## Publisher checklist
- [ ] `id` is unique, lowercase, and hyphenated
- [ ] Definition is compact, clear, and educational
- [ ] Specialized language is explained without flattening nuance
- [ ] Related IDs exist
- [ ] Optional `learnLink` points to a real deeper page
