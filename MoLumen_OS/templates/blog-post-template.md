- [ ] Description accurately summarizes the article
- [ ] Dates/category/tags are correct
- [ ] Voice is warm authority without mystique: intelligent, grounded, candid, curious, conversational, and agency-first

- [ ] Claims that require verification are sourced
- [ ] Natural internal links are included where useful
