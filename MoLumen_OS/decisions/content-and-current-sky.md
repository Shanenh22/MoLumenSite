# Content and Current Sky Decisions

- MoLumen should be a durable knowledge source, not a volume-content farm.
- The canonical voice is warm authority without mystique: intelligent, grounded, candid, curious, technically informed in plain language, conversational, and agency-first.
- Mo's practice is collaborative astrological synthesis. Technique, intuition, lived experience, and conversation inform one another; intuition can suggest where to look but does not end the inquiry.
- Wonder is part of the brand, but natural-science experience is not evidence that proves astrology. Avoid scientific-sounding causation claims presented as established fact.
- Editorial work separates verified astronomical facts, tradition/doctrine, contemporary practice, and Mo's interpretation while preserving reader agency.
- Free content teaches vocabulary, structure, context, limits, shared cycles, and general interpretation. It should not become a free automated personal astrologer.
- Content roles stay distinct: Explore teaches; Current Sky observes/interprets shared cycles; Horoscopes provide a broad rising-sign whole-sign-house lens; Blog develops durable ideas; Newsletter is broader monthly correspondence; readings provide individualized synthesis.
- Preferred visitor journey: Learn → deepen → observe Mo's thinking → understand the reading experience → identify the right reading → book. Page-level CTAs should respect current visitor intent instead of defaulting to Book.
- The newsletter is a **monthly note from Mo**. "The Sky This Month" is one recurring section, not the newsletter's full identity.
- Current Sky uses researched event data with authoritative sources, date/time-zone verification, and cross-checking for important dates.
- Current Sky maintains a rolling future horizon; any specific coverage endpoint is current state, not permanent policy.
- Do not flood Current Sky with every minor aspect; preserve the established editorial scope.
- Rising-sign guidance says where to look structurally; it does not replace whole-chart timing, context, or individualized interpretation.
- The evergreen Birth Time guide and the worksheet/lead-magnet track are separate concerns: education stays useful without requiring a download, while derived PDFs should be generated from authoritative source content.
