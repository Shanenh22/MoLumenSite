# MoLumen Product Charter

MoLumen is an authority website and publishing platform for Mo Lumen Astrology. Its primary business outcome is qualified paid reading bookings. It should also grow an owned audience, teach clearly, support ongoing publishing, and remain straightforward to maintain.

## Product principles
- Build trust before asking for conversion.
- Help visitors understand Mo's approach and choose the right reading.
- Prefer clear, specific language over mystical vagueness or hype.
- Preserve reader agency; avoid deterministic claims.
- Keep valuable content on the owned website while using newsletter/video platforms as distribution and trust channels.
- Favor durable information architecture and maintainability over volume publishing.
- Add products or platforms only when an active requirement justifies them.

## Primary journey
Discover → understand Mo → choose reading → book → pay → prepare → reading → continue relationship.

## Success hierarchy
1. Qualified completed paid reading bookings.
2. Newsletter/audience growth and repeat engagement.
3. Search/answer-engine discoverability and useful educational depth.
4. Maintainable publishing and operations.

## Owner gates
Owner input is required for credentials/account actions, legal claims, irreversible infrastructure actions, novel paid products, and brand decisions that cannot be inferred from approved materials.

Engineering behavior, evidence rules, validation, and context-loading policy live in root `AGENTS.md` and should not be duplicated here.
