# MoLumen Product Charter

MoLumen is the authority website and publishing platform for **Mo Lumen Astrology**. The confirmed legal name is **Mo Lumen Astrological Services**. The primary business outcome is qualified paid reading bookings. The site should also grow an owned audience, teach clearly, support ongoing publishing, and remain straightforward to maintain.

## Core audience
Thoughtful adults at meaningful crossroads: people navigating a decision, relationship, transition, recurring pattern, or deeper question who want perspective without being told what to believe or what to do.

They tend to value intelligent explanation, technical knowledge in plain language, candor, compassion, curiosity, and retained agency. They may be curious about astrology without wanting guru culture, fatalism, or mystical performance.

## Product promise
Mo offers clarity without surrendering agency. A reading gives the client another map; the client still drives.

Mo's practice is a collaborative act of astrological synthesis. Technique, intuition, lived experience, and conversation inform one another. The chart suggests patterns and possibilities; the client supplies the life the chart belongs to.

## Product principles
- Build trust before asking for conversion.
- Let visitors encounter Mo's mind, not just her biography or credentials.
- Preserve wonder without using science as proof of astrology.
- Prefer clear, specific language over mystical vagueness, hype, belief tests, or guru language.
- Preserve reader agency; avoid deterministic claims, doom, certainty, or instructions disguised as astrology.
- Keep valuable content on the owned website while using newsletter/video platforms as distribution and relationship channels.
- Teach generously without turning the site into a free automated personal astrologer.
- Match each page's CTA to the visitor's current intent rather than sending everyone directly to Book.
- Favor durable information architecture and maintainability over volume publishing.
- Add products or platforms only when an active requirement justifies them.

## Content ecosystem
- **Explore Astrology** teaches the language, structure, and limits of astrology.
- **Current Sky** observes and interprets shared cycles.
- **Horoscopes** provide a broad rising-sign whole-sign-house lens with explicit limits.
- **Blog** develops durable ideas and shows how Mo thinks.
- **Newsletter** is monthly correspondence from Mo; "The Sky This Month" is one recurring section, not the whole product.
- **Readings** provide individualized synthesis, judgment, context, and conversation.

## Preferred visitor journey
Learn → deepen → observe Mo's thinking → understand the reading experience → identify the right reading → book.

Not every visitor enters at the beginning, so each substantial page should answer "what next?" intelligently from its own role.

## Success hierarchy
1. Qualified completed paid reading bookings.
2. Newsletter/audience growth and repeat engagement.
3. Search/answer-engine discoverability and useful educational depth.
4. Maintainable publishing and operations.

## Owner gates
Owner input is required for credentials/account actions, legal claims, legal identity changes, irreversible infrastructure actions, novel paid products, service/pricing/eligibility changes, and brand decisions that cannot be inferred from approved materials.

Engineering behavior, evidence rules, validation, and context-loading policy live in root `AGENTS.md` and should not be duplicated here.
