# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO

Build a durable knowledge source, not a volume-content farm. Search visibility should follow useful, trustworthy content and clear entities rather than keyword padding.

## Core topic clusters
- readings and how readings work
- birth chart basics
- planets, signs, houses, aspects, angles, dignity, patterns
- timing/transits
- Current Sky
- lunations/eclipses
- rising-sign horoscopes and their limits
- methodology/sources
- birth time and preparation
- Mo's approach, training, and published reasoning

## Content roles
Keep the ecosystem legible to people and machines:
- Explore teaches astrology's language, structure, and limits.
- Current Sky documents and interprets shared cycles.
- Horoscopes use a broad rising-sign whole-sign-house lens, not individualized forecasting.
- Blog develops durable ideas and reasoning.
- Newsletter is ongoing monthly correspondence, broader than its "The Sky This Month" section.
- Readings provide individualized synthesis and conversation.

Do not create near-duplicate pages merely to capture adjacent queries.

## Every publishable item should support where applicable
- unique, accurate title and meta description
- self-consistent canonical
- one clear H1 and semantic heading order
- useful contextual internal links
- breadcrumbs
- truthful structured data
- image dimensions/alt where images add value
- Open Graph/social metadata
- truthful publication/update dates
- author/entity information
- an intent-appropriate next step

## Structured-data truth
Schema must describe what the page actually is. An article about a celestial event is not itself a hosted `Event`. Do not use the astronomical event date as an article publication date unless it truly is the publication date. Do not fabricate upload dates, event attendance modes, locations, organizers, reviews, prices, or other machine-readable facts.

## Entity consistency
Public brand: **Mo Lumen Astrology**. Confirmed legal name: **Mo Lumen Astrological Services**. Mo is an **Astrologer**; do not use schema to narrow her identity to one astrological school when the public practice is broadly studied and synthesis-oriented.

## AEO/GEO
Favor direct answers, concise definitions, entity consistency, dated/sourced facts, clear authorship, semantic HTML, explicit limits, and context-rich internal links. Explain what is known, what is astrological interpretation, and what requires an individual chart/context.

Do not manufacture FAQs solely for schema, pad pages with repetitive definitions, or turn free educational content into automated personalized astrology merely to chase answer-engine visibility.
