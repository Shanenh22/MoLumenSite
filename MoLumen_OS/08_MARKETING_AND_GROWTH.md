# Marketing and Growth

## Audience and outcome
The primary audience is a thoughtful adult at a meaningful crossroads who wants deeper perspective without surrendering judgment or agency. Growth should attract more of the right people, not maximize traffic indiscriminately.

The primary business outcome is a qualified paid reading. Secondary outcomes are newsletter relationship, repeat engagement, referrals, and future learning/product demand.

## Visitor journey
Preferred journey: Learn → deepen → observe Mo's thinking → understand the reading experience → identify the right reading → book.

Visitors can enter anywhere, so every substantial page should offer an intent-aware next step rather than defaulting to Book.

Examples:
- Explore / Start Here → deeper learning or Current Sky.
- Current Sky / Horoscopes → related sky content, calendar, newsletter, or Reading Finder when the question becomes personal.
- Blog → topic-relevant next step.
- About / Approach / Credentials / Testimonials → How Readings Work or Reading Finder.
- Readings → compare, identify fit, then book.

## Positioning
Mo offers clarity without surrendering agency. The differentiator is not mystique or prediction theater; it is technically grounded, conversational synthesis that takes the chart seriously and the client's lived experience seriously.

Use credibility, wonder, transparency, and reasoning together. Do not over-index on credentials, skepticism rebuttals, fear, certainty, or conversion pressure.

## Newsletter / Kit
The newsletter is a monthly note from Mo and a relationship channel, not a mini Current Sky product. "The Sky This Month" is one recurring section.

Use it to nurture curious visitors, share Current Sky observations, questions, patterns, connections, nature/biology observations, useful new work, lead magnets, and future launches when there is something real to announce.

Do not create artificial scarcity or promise future topics/products before they exist.

## YouTube and social
Use video to show Mo's personality, expertise, warmth, teaching style, and way of thinking. Support articles and Current Sky without moving the site's most valuable knowledge entirely onto third-party platforms.

Keep useful summaries/transcripts and durable context on the owned website where appropriate.

## Free versus paid
Free content should be generous about vocabulary, fundamentals, context, limits, and general interpretation. Paid value comes from individualized synthesis, judgment, application, and conversation.

MoLumen should be an excellent dictionary, textbook, guide, and place to observe Mo thinking. It should not become a free automated astrologer.

## Analytics
Track business questions, not vanity metrics:
- Reading Finder starts/completions
- recommendation views
- booking CTA clicks
- service deep-links
- Cal embed loads
- completed bookings where observable
- newsletter intent/completion where observable
- video activation
- outbound video/social clicks
- content paths that meaningfully lead toward deeper engagement or qualified booking

Never send birth data, free-text personal questions, rising-sign preference, or other sensitive/personal reading context into analytics.
